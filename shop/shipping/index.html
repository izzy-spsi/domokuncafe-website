Merch shipping

Shipping and pickup.

This page is for Domo Cafe merch. It is not how you order food. Food pickup and delivery stay on Toast, DoorDash, and Grubhub.

Where we ship

We ship to the 50 US states by USPS Ground Advantage. Checkout only accepts a United States address. We do not ship outside the US.

What it costs

  • $4.95 flat shipping for an order with 1 item.
  • Free shipping on an order with 2 or more items.
  • Free cafe pickup, any quantity.

How fast

Ships within 2 business days after the order is paid. Transit time is USPS Ground Advantage after that.

Cafe pickup

Pickup is free at Domo Cafe, 8340 La Palma Ave Ste R, Buena Park, CA 90620. Half of each set is stocked at the cafe for pickup, and half is stocked to ship. If one location is out, the other may still be available.
