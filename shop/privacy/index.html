Merch privacy

Privacy.

The merch shop is guest checkout. You do not create a Domo Cafe account.

Payment

Stripe hosts checkout and processes your card, wallets, email, phone, and billing details. Domo Cafe does not store your card number. Stripe’s own privacy policy covers that payment page.

What we keep

After you pay, Stripe tells our shop server the order: which sets, ship or cafe pickup, the amount, and the email and phone you entered. We use that to pack the order and to put stock back if Stripe reports a refund. A sold-out “notify me” email is stored only so we can write if that set is made again. We do not sell this list.

Your cart

The cart stays in this browser (local storage) until you pay or clear it. It is not an account.

Questions

Email info@domokuncafe.com about a merch order or to ask us to delete a notify-me email.
