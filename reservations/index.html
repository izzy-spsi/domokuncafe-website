Before you book

  • Please book for your full party size, including children.
  • We'll hold your table for 15 minutes after your reservation time; if you're running late, please contact us, after that seating may depend on availability.
  • Please arrive with your complete party so we can seat everyone together.
  • If your plans change, please cancel or update your reservation as soon as possible.
  • For large parties or special seating requests, please contact the cafe before booking; specific tables cannot be guaranteed.
  • Walk-ins are welcome; our online waitlist is separate from reservations and does not hold a table for a future time.
