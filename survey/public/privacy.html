Survey privacy notice

This notice describes the guest survey on this site. It is the working notice for the survey module. Cafe management still needs to approve the public privacy policy, retention settings, and any email provider before a live promotion.

What the reward requires

You can complete the survey and receive a reward without giving your name, phone number, email, or payment information. The survey does not ask for a street address, birth date, or GPS location. A home ZIP code, or a city and country, is optional.

Optional email

After the survey, you can choose to sign up for Domo Cafe emails. That signup is separate. Skipping it, closing it, or a signup error does not remove a reward that was already issued. We record the permission text you were shown and the time you agreed. We do not treat a survey, a reward, or an earlier visit as permission to email you.

What staff can see

Staff who redeem a reward see the code, the offer, the deadline, and the instructions for that reward. They do not see your survey answers or your email on the redemption screen. Managers can see survey reports. Subscriber exports are limited to an owner or administrator.

How answers are described

Reports describe people who chose to answer. They are not a count of unique customers, and an email match is not treated as proof of one person or a whole party. Because an optional email can be linked to the response that signed up, we do not promise that every response stays permanently anonymous.

Retention and deletion

The proposed defaults, pending owner approval, are 24 months for survey and reward records, 90 days for abandoned attempt metadata, and 30 days for application logs. Consent and unsubscribe history are kept so we can honor a later opt-out. To ask for contact deletion, talk to a manager at the cafe. Deletion removes the email and unlinks it from responses. Aggregate counts and the audit record of the deletion remain.
