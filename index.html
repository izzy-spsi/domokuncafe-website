Need to know

Quick answers.

Still curious? Send us a note and our crew will help out.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes! Walk-ins are welcome. On busy weekends, arriving early is your best bet. For large groups (8+), call ahead at (415) 360-3666 so we can ready a table.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available in the large Buena Park Downtown lot.

Can I celebrate a birthday here?

Absolutely — we love birthdays. For celebrations with more than 8 guests, we offer birthday packages. Call us at (415) 360-3666 or email info@domokuncafe.com and we’ll set you up.
