What it is

Dinner that does some good.

Domo Community Tuesdays are a recurring fundraiser night at our Buena Park cafe. Your group gets the spotlight. Your people get a table. The neighborhood gets a reason to show up.

One org, one Tuesday

We feature a single school, team, club, or nonprofit so guests know exactly who they are supporting that night.

Code or QR tracking

Supporters mention your event code or scan your QR when they order so qualifying visits can be attributed to your night.

About 15% back

Domo targets about 15% of qualifying sales for the featured organization. Final rules are confirmed at booking.

Built for Buena Park groups

If you can rally families, teammates, classmates, or neighbors for a meal, you can host a Community Tuesday. Walk-ins stay welcome — this is a promoted night, not a private buyout.

Who it’s for

Schools, teams, and neighbors.

How the night works

Simple on purpose.

You promote. Guests come hungry. We run the cafe like a regular Tuesday — with your cause on the table tents and tickets.

01

Request a Tuesday

Pick an open date, tell us who you are, and send the form. We review and confirm subject to approval.

02

Get your code

Once booked, you receive an event code and QR cue to share with families, teammates, and followers.

03

Rally your people

You spread the word. Guests visit Domo Cafe, order, and mention the code or scan the QR.

04

We tally the night

Qualifying attributed sales are counted after the event. About 15% is the target donation share.

Who does what

We host. You rally.

No kitchen takeover, no ticket sales to manage, and no need to staff the cafe. Your job is the invite list.

What Domo provides

  • A featured Tuesday on the cafe calendar
  • An event code and QR cue for tracking
  • In-cafe mentions so guests know who the night supports
  • Regular menu, seating, and hospitality
  • A tally of attributed qualifying sales after the night

What your group provides

  • A primary contact who can confirm details
  • Promotion to families, teammates, and socials
  • A short blurb and logo for your Community profile
  • A clear fundraising purpose we can share
  • A realistic headcount so we can plan the floor

What guests do

  • Come to 8340 La Palma Ave on your Tuesday
  • Order food or drinks as they normally would
  • Give the code or scan the QR at order time
  • Dine in or take out — walk-ins stay welcome
  • Share the night so more neighbors can help
Qualifying sales

About 15%, confirmed at booking.

We describe the program as about 15% of qualifying sales so families know what to expect. Qualifying sales are attributed food and beverage orders tied to your code or QR. Merchandise, gift cards, and other exclusions may apply.

This is not a private-event minimum or a guaranteed dollar amount. The more supporters who come and use the code, the more your night can raise. Final donation rules, timing, and any exclusions are confirmed when we book your Tuesday.

No surprises for PTA parents

You will not be asked to buy out the restaurant. Guests pay for their own meals. Domo runs the cafe. Your group promotes the night and we follow up after with the attributed result.

FAQ

            
Before you call.

          

          
Still unsure? Email info@domokuncafe.com or call (415) 360-3666.

        

        

          

            Do we have to buy out the cafe?
            
No. Community Tuesday is a promoted night, not a private party. Regular guests can still walk in. Your supporters come, order, and use your code or QR.

          

          

            What counts as a qualifying sale?
            
Attributed food and beverage orders tied to your night’s code or QR. About 15% of those qualifying sales is the target donation share. Merchandise and other items may be excluded. Final rules are confirmed at booking.

          

          

            Is the 15% guaranteed?
            
We talk about the program as about 15% of qualifying sales. The exact percentage, timing, and exclusions are confirmed when we book your Tuesday so there are no surprises for your treasurer.

          

          

            Can we request more than one Tuesday?
            
Yes — select every open Tuesday that could work. We will confirm one featured night unless we agree on more than one date.

          

          

            How do guests make sure the order counts?
            
They mention your event code or scan your QR when they order in cafe or through the participating order path we confirm at booking. Orders without the code may not be attributed.

          

          

            When should we request?
            
As early as you can — especially around school calendar busy weeks. Open dates on this page can still be declined or moved if the cafe already has a hold that is not listed yet.

          

          

            Will our group get a webpage?
            
Yes. A Domo Community profile per organization is part of the program. Full profiles are coming soon; booked groups still receive a code and QR for the night itself. See the BPHS Tennis sample.

          

          

            What if our date is already reserved?
            
Pick another open Tuesday, or email us with a few backups. Reserved dates on this page are held for another group or an internal cafe hold.
