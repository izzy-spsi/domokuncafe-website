Halloween in Buena Park

A spooky-cute Halloween cafe in Orange County

Looking for Halloween things to do in Buena Park that are more cute than creepy? From October 1 through October 31, 2026, Domo Cafe celebrates Domoween, our Halloween season starring Domo, the beloved NHK character, dressed up as a witch, a mummy, Frankenstein's monster and a jack-o'-lantern, with friends Mr. Usagi and Tashanna in their pumpkin looks.

Domo Cafe is a Halloween-themed cafe stop for the whole month: come hungry for Japanese-inspired comfort food and playful drinks, snap photos with friends, and check the October events calendar for Domoween happenings. It's an easy Halloween outing for friends and families across Orange County and the greater Los Angeles area.

You'll find us at 8340 La Palma Ave in Buena Park, with free parking in the large Buena Park Downtown lot. Walk-ins are welcome, and you can order online for pickup or delivery.

Good to know

Domoween FAQ

When is Domoween at Domo Cafe?

October 1 through October 31, 2026, during regular cafe hours: 11am–8pm Monday to Friday and 10am–9pm Saturday and Sunday. Hours may vary on holidays.

Where is Domo Cafe?

8340 La Palma Ave, Buena Park, CA 90620, in Orange County. Free parking is available in the large Buena Park Downtown lot.

Is Domoween scary?

Not at all. Domoween is all about cute, not creepy: Domo and friends in their Halloween costumes, with friendly ghosts and jack-o'-lanterns.

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome. On busy weekends, arriving early is your best bet. For groups of 8 or more, call (415) 360-3666 so we can ready a table.

Can I order for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order online for pickup or delivery through Toast.

What happens after October?

Domoween wraps up on October 31 and returns next October. Follow @domokuncafe on Instagram and TikTok for news.
